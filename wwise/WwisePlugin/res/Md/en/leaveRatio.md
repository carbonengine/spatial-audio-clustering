## Leave Ratio

Angle at which an input starts to leave its cluster, as a multiple of the Join Angle. An input leaves only after it has been farther than Join Angle × Leave Ratio from its cluster for the Dwell Time.

The gap between the join and leave angles stops inputs from switching back and forth between clusters.

**Note**: When the Max Outputs limit is reached, a new input with no cluster within the Join Angle joins the nearest cluster within Join Angle × Leave Ratio.

Units: × Join Angle
